About Waypoint

A veteran who learned the VA system the hard way — so you don't have to.

The Long Way Around

Seven years, one claim, a lot of lessons.

Tim founded Waypoint Veteran Group after spending seven years navigating his own VA disability claim — from his first, confusing attempt at filing, through denials, appeals, and finally understanding what the VA actually needed to see in order to approve a claim. Along the way he learned what most veterans learn only after a denial letter arrives: that a claim isn't won or lost on how much a veteran has suffered, but on how clearly the medical evidence connects that suffering to military service.

What started as figuring out his own paperwork turned into helping the veteran next to him. Then a few more. Word got around a Triad barbershop, then a VFW post, then a unit reunion — and True North grew entirely on word of mouth, one veteran referring the next.

"I'm not trying to be the biggest firm out there. I'm trying to give the guy sitting where I was seven years ago the thing nobody gave me — a straight answer about what the VA actually needs to see, from someone who's been on the other side of that desk as a veteran, not a stranger reading off a script."

— Tim, Founder, Waypoint Veteran Group

Where We Are Today

Growing carefully, on reputation — not ad spend.

Waypoint currently operates on referrals: veterans who worked with Tim telling other veterans. That's deliberate. This industry has earned a lot of well-founded skepticism from large, aggressively-marketed claims companies, and Tim would rather grow slowly with veterans who trust him than fast with veterans who don't.

A note on accreditation: Tim is not currently a VA-accredited claims agent. His application for accreditation is pending. Until that accreditation is granted, Waypoint's role is educational and advisory — helping veterans understand the process and organize their evidence — and does not include preparing, presenting, or representing claims before the VA. We'll update this page the moment that changes.
