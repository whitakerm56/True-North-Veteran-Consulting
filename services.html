VA Claims Support

Understand the process. Build a stronger file.

Most claims aren't denied because a veteran wasn't disabled — they're denied because the file didn't clearly show the connection between the condition and military service. Waypoint helps you understand that standard and organize your file to meet it.

  • Plain-language explanation of the VA rating and claims process
  • Help identifying which conditions may qualify and why
  • Evidence organization — service records, medical history, supporting statements
  • Guidance on what a strong claim file typically includes
Important: Tim is not currently a VA-accredited claims agent (accreditation application pending). Waypoint provides education and organizational guidance only — we do not prepare, present, file, or represent claims on a veteran's behalf before the VA. Veterans always retain the right to file their own claim or work with an accredited Veteran Service Organization (VSO), claims agent, or attorney, many of which offer help at no cost.
Medical Evaluation Services

Independent medical opinions, when your claim needs one.

Some claims — especially ones involving secondary conditions or a connection that isn't obvious from the record — benefit from an independent medical opinion. True North connects veterans with qualified, independent medical professionals for exactly that purpose.

Independent Medical Opinions

Access to independent, licensed medical professionals who can review your case and provide a documented opinion when medically appropriate.

Medical Record Reviews

A thorough review of your existing medical records to identify what's supporting your claim — and what's missing.

Nexus Opinion Coordination

Coordination with independent providers who can evaluate whether a clear connection exists between a condition and military service.

DBQs, When Appropriate

Access to Disability Benefits Questionnaires completed by qualified providers, when medically indicated for your specific claim.

Provider Network

A growing network of qualified, independent medical professionals

Waypoint works with independent medical professionals across several specialties. As the network grows, veterans get access to the right type of provider for their specific condition — not a one-size-fits-all evaluation.

Nurse Practitioners

Independent evaluations and record reviews for a broad range of physical conditions.

Physicians

Physician-level opinions for complex or contested medical questions.

Mental Health Providers

Licensed mental health professionals for PTSD, depression, anxiety, and related secondary conditions.

This network is actively growing. If you're a licensed provider interested in independent VA-related evaluations, reach out.
