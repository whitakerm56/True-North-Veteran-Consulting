The Mission

Veterans deserve a clear path to the evidence that supports their claim.

The VA disability process is dense, slow, and unforgiving of missing paperwork. Most veterans aren't taught how to build a claim — they learn it the hard way, one denial letter at a time. True North exists to close that gap: plain-language education, organized evidence, and access to independent medical professionals who can provide the documentation a strong claim depends on.

Who we help

Filing a first claim

Veterans who don't know where to start and want the process explained in plain terms.

Appealing a denial

Veterans who were denied or under-rated and need to understand what evidence was missing.

Needing medical evidence

Veterans whose claim depends on an independent medical opinion connecting a condition to service.

How We Help

Three ways we support your claim

Education & Process Guidance

Understand how the VA rates disabilities, what evidence actually moves a claim forward, and what to expect at each stage — explained by someone who's been through it himself.

Evidence Organization

We help you gather, organize, and present your service records, medical history, and supporting statements in a way that's clear and complete before you file.

Medical Evaluation Access

Connect with independent, qualified medical professionals for record reviews, nexus opinions, and DBQs when medically appropriate.

True North is a consulting practice, not a law firm or accredited claims agent (accreditation application pending) — see our Services page for exactly what that means for you.
