Why Choose Waypoint Veteran Group?

Finding your way through the VA claims process can be overwhelming.

Our mission is simple: help veterans understand the process, organize their evidence, and move forward with confidence. We believe every veteran deserves honest guidance, transparent communication, and someone who understands the journey firsthand.

Meet Your Consultant

Hi, I'm Tim.

I'm a U.S. Navy veteran who understands how frustrating and confusing the VA disability system can be. After navigating the claims process myself and spending years studying VA regulations, medical evidence, and disability evaluations, I founded Waypoint Veteran Group to help other veterans make informed decisions about their benefits.

My goal isn't to make unrealistic promises. It's to provide honest guidance, personalized support, and help you present your strongest claim.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you guarantee a disability rating?

No. Every VA claim is decided by the Department of Veterans Affairs based on the evidence submitted.

How much does it cost?

Every case is unique. During your free consultation, we'll discuss your needs and explain any applicable fees before you decide to move forward.

Do you help with denied claims?

Yes. We can review your situation and discuss available options that may be appropriate.

Can you review my medical records?

Yes. We'll help organize and review your documentation to identify strengths and potential gaps.

Is Waypoint Veteran Group VA accredited?

Tim's VA accreditation application is currently pending. Until it's approved, Waypoint provides education and organizational guidance and does not prepare, present, or represent claims before the VA.
