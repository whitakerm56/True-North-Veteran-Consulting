How It Works

A straightforward process, explained up front — no surprises.

01

Request a Consultation

Fill out a short form describing your situation — your branch of service, where your claim currently stands, and what you're trying to accomplish. This takes about five minutes and there's no cost or obligation.

02

Records Reviewed

Tim reviews what you already have — service records, medical history, prior VA decisions — and identifies what's supporting your claim and what's missing.

03

Matched With the Right Professional

If your claim would benefit from an independent medical opinion, you're connected with the appropriate provider from our network — not a generic, one-size-fits-all evaluation.

04

Medical Evaluation & Guidance Provided

You receive clear guidance on your file and, when appropriate, documentation from an independent medical professional — ready for you or your accredited representative to submit.

Common Questions

What veterans usually ask first

How long does the process take?

It depends on your situation. An initial consultation and records review typically takes one to two weeks. If a medical evaluation is needed, timing depends on the independent provider's availability.

Do I need a medical evaluation for every claim?

No. Some claims are already well-supported by existing service and medical records. An independent medical evaluation is recommended only when it would genuinely strengthen the connection between a condition and military service.

Will True North file my claim for me?

Not currently. Tim's VA accreditation application is pending. True North provides education, evidence organization, and access to independent medical professionals — veterans file their own claims or work with an accredited VSO, agent, or attorney to do so.
