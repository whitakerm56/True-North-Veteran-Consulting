Waypoint Veteran Group is a private, veteran-owned consulting practice based in the Piedmont Triad of North Carolina. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs or any other government agency. VA disability decisions are made solely by the Department of Veterans Affairs, and no specific outcome, rating, or benefit is ever guaranteed. Tim is not currently a VA-accredited claims agent — his accreditation application is pending — and Waypoint does not prepare, present, or represent veterans’ claims before the VA. Our role is educational and advisory: helping veterans understand the process, organize their medical evidence, and connect with independent, qualified medical professionals.
